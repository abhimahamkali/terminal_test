# Captions — Small dogs, actually

Every word is taken from the slide it belongs to (and the slides from tendsmall.com).

## Full (title + takeaway)

**00** — Small dogs. Small system. Every bite, sip, and treat has to do more.

**01** — Small dogs have less room for error. Smaller bodies. Tighter margins. Less buffer.

**02** — Digestion isn't what you see. What matters isn't what goes in. It's what makes it through.

**03** — Hydration isn't moisture. Food plays a part. It doesn't carry the whole thing.

**04** — Calories in ≠ Calories used. Unabsorbed calories still count on the label. They don't count for the dog.

**05** — Most picky eating is learned. The food is rarely the problem. The pattern around it is.

**06** — The coat is a signal. Treat the coat, treat the surface. Treat the cause, the coat follows.

**07** — Five systems. One mistake. Different problems. Same shape.

**08** — Treat everything the same way, you miss the system. What's missing isn't ingredients. It's a model of how the body actually works.

**09** — Read the dog. Not the chart. This is the evidence. Everything else is a proxy.

**10** — Your dog isn't separate systems. Treat any one in isolation — incomplete answer. Everything works together. Or nothing quite does.

**11** — What matters most. It's the only number that counts.

**12** — Most food is enough. The distance between enough and right isn't dramatic. It's just consistent. Every day. For years.

**13** — When food fits the system — Everything downstream settles.
