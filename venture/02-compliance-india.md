# 02 — India Compliance, Licensing & Regulatory

> Verified against current rules as of **September 2026**. Two things changed recently and both matter to you:
> the FSSAI turnover thresholds were raised (effective 1 April 2026) and GST on your likely HSN head
> dropped from 12% to 5% (effective 22 September 2025). Details and sources below.
> Have your CA confirm HSN classification in writing before you print MRPs.

---

## 1. Entity structure

| Option | Setup cost | When it's right | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proprietorship | ~₹0 (just GST + FSSAI) | Testing only, no partners, no outside money | Fine for a 3-month pilot |
| LLP | ₹7,000–12,000 | Two+ partners, no equity fundraising planned | Adequate, but hotels and modern trade read it as small |
| **Private Limited** | ₹8,000–18,000 via SPICe+ | You want to raise, add co-founders, export, or sell into organised chains | **Recommended** |

**Why Pvt Ltd for you specifically:** hotel and quick-commerce vendor onboarding both favour incorporated entities; you need clean cap-table hygiene if Deepthi/Saketh or anyone else ever comes in; and export (your Phase 3) is much cleaner from a company. Incorporate via **SPICe+** on the MCA portal — you get CIN, PAN, TAN, EPFO, ESIC and a bank account reference in one flow.

Also register for **Udyam (MSME)** immediately after incorporation. It is free, takes 15 minutes, and it unlocks: 50% trademark fee discount, protection under the MSMED Act's 45-day payment rule (**directly useful against slow-paying hotels**), priority-sector lending rates, and eligibility for state food-processing subsidies.

## 2. FSSAI — the licence question (recently changed in your favour)

Thresholds effective **1 April 2026** (Licensing & Registration Amendment Regulations, 2026):

| Tier | Annual turnover | Indicative fee |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic Registration** | up to **₹1.5 crore** (was ₹12 lakh) | ~₹100/year |
| **State Licence** | ₹1.5 crore – ₹50 crore (was ₹12L–₹20cr) | ~₹3,000–5,000/year |
| **Central Licence** | above ₹50 crore (was ₹20 crore) | ~₹7,500/year |

At your planned scale (~₹50–60 lakh/year) you now fall inside **Basic Registration**, which is dramatically cheaper and faster than the old regime would have required.

**Three caveats before you rely on that:**

1. **Production-capacity criterion.** Historically, registration eligibility for a manufacturer also carried a **capacity cap (~100 kg/day)** independent of turnover. At 6 kg/day you are far inside it — but confirm the capacity criterion survived the 2026 amendment before you file. Get this in writing from your consultant.
2. **Your B2B customers will ask for more than the law requires.** Hotel purchase departments, corporate pantry vendors and quick-commerce onboarding routinely demand a **State Licence** (and increasingly **ISO 22000 or HACCP**) in vendor registration, regardless of what FSSAI minimally requires of you. A Basic Registration certificate will get you rejected by a Minerva or Avira purchase desk. **Take the State Licence voluntarily.** It costs ~₹5,000/year and removes a sales objection worth lakhs.
3. **Export requires a Central Licence.** When you get to Phase 3 (overseas supply), you must upgrade to Central regardless of turnover, plus IEC and APEDA. Plan for it; don't be surprised by it.

**Net recommendation:** apply for a **State Licence** under the manufacturer category, even though you're legally eligible for Registration. Treat the extra ₹5,000/year as a sales cost, not a compliance cost.

### Product category — keep it boring, on purpose

This is where your "don't be a novel product, easy to register" instinct pays off. Your options:

| Route | FSSAI treatment | Difficulty | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mixed masala / spice blend** | Standardised food, spices & condiments | Easiest | Viable, but a poor fit for a tea bag |
| **Mixed condiment / seasoning** | Standardised | Easy | **Best fit** |
| **Soup / broth preparation** | Standardised (soups & broths) | Easy | Also fine; check HSN implications |
| **Proprietary Food** | No product approval needed, but must use only permitted ingredients and declare the nearest category | Easy | Fallback if your blend won't fit a standard |
| **Health supplement / nutraceutical** | HS&N Regulations 2022 — dossiers, claim substantiation, restricted ingredients | **Hard** | **Avoid. This is what a "gut health" claim drags you into.** |

**The trap to stay out of:** the moment you make a physiological claim ("aids digestion", "debloats", "appetite booster", "gut health"), a regulator can reasonably argue you are selling a health supplement, not a seasoning. That triggers a far heavier regime, kills your speed advantage, and is the single biggest self-inflicted risk in your plan. Keep the product a **seasoning/soup preparation** on paper and do the gut-health work in *culture*, not in claims. See `00-strategy.md` §3 for the exact language swaps.

### Claims law you must actually follow

Governed by the **FSS (Advertising and Claims) Regulations, 2018**:
- **Nutrient content claims** ("0 g sugar", "no added sugar", "caffeine-free", "low calorie", "no MSG") are permitted and only need to be factually true and within defined thresholds. **These are your entire marketing arsenal. They are free and they are safe.**
- **Health / function claims** ("improves digestion") require scientific substantiation you can produce on demand. Don't.
- **"Natural", "fresh", "pure", "original"** are each separately restricted — don't use them casually on pack.
- **Influencer content is your advertising.** ASCI's influencer guidelines plus the Consumer Protection Act's misleading-advertisement provisions mean a paid creator's claim is attributed to you, with liability on the advertiser. Issue every creator a written brief limiting them to sensory + subtraction language, and keep the signed brief on file. This is a five-minute process that protects you from a real penalty.

## 3. Labelling — Food Safety and Standards (Labelling & Display) Regulations, 2020

Mandatory on the retail pack:
- Name of the food and the category
- Complete ingredient list, descending order by weight
- **Nutritional information** per 100 g and per serving (energy, protein, carbs, total sugars, added sugars, fat, saturated fat, trans fat, sodium)
- **Allergen declaration** — see the asafoetida/gluten flag in `01-product-packaging.md` §5
- **Green veg mark**
- **FSSAI logo + licence/registration number**
- Net quantity
- Lot/batch number
- Date of manufacture + "Best before"
- Name and complete address of the manufacturer / FBO
- Country of origin
- Consumer care contact (phone/email)
- Cooking/serving instructions

On a 3 g envelope this will not fit legibly. Standard and legal solution: **full declaration on the outer carton, minimal mandatory set on the individual envelope.** Confirm your exact carve-out with your labelling consultant — the regulations provide relief for small packages, but the threshold and the reduced mandatory set are specific and worth getting right once.

## 4. Legal Metrology — and the institutional exemption you should use

The **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011** govern MRP, net quantity, importer/packer details and unit-sale price on retail packs.

**Key exemption:** packages meant for **institutional or industrial consumers** are exempt from the retail declarations (including MRP) if marked accordingly. So your hotel/banquet/corporate cartons should be printed:

> **For institutional use only — not for retail sale**

This is genuinely useful to you:
- No MRP on the hotel SKU, so hotel pricing stays confidential and you can price per account without a public reference price undercutting your ₹249 retail box.
- Fewer declarations on the custom-branded hotel envelope, which eases the artwork problem in `01-product-packaging.md` §4.

You will still need a **Legal Metrology packer/manufacturer registration** for the retail SKU. Cheap, state-level, do it alongside FSSAI.

## 5. GST

**Rate — confirm the head, then the rate follows:**

| Likely HSN | Description | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| **2103** | Sauces; **mixed condiments and mixed seasonings** | **5%** — reduced from 12% by the 56th GST Council, Notification 9/2025-CT(Rate), effective 22 Sep 2025 |
| **2104** | **Soups and broths and preparations therefor** | Post-Sept-2025 sources point to 5%, but public sources still conflict (some show 18%). **Get this in writing from your CA.** |
| 0910 | Spices, mixed masala | 5% |

**Action:** classify under **HSN 2103 as a mixed condiment/seasoning** if your formulation supports it. It is the best-documented 5% head, and it aligns with the "boring, standardised, easy to register" FSSAI route above. Do not build your price list until the HSN is confirmed in writing — the difference between 5% and 18% is ~₹30/box of retail margin and it will silently destroy your quick-commerce economics if you guess wrong.

**Registration and mechanics:**
- **GST registration is mandatory** for you regardless of turnover — you'll be supplying B2B across states (hotels in Vizag/Tirupati if Minerva) and selling via e-commerce operators. Quick commerce platforms will not onboard you without a GSTIN.
- Register for **e-invoicing** when applicable; at your scale initially not required, but quick-commerce and hotel chains may demand e-invoices anyway.
- **Input tax credit** — your packaging (laminate, cartons, labels) and job-work charges all carry GST you can claim. Keep the co-packer's invoices clean; this is real money at your margins.
- **E-way bills** for consignments above the threshold when shipping to hotels in other states.
- **TCS by e-commerce operators** — Blinkit/Zepto will deduct TCS; reconcile it monthly or you'll lose it.

## 6. Trademark

- File in **Class 30** (spices, condiments, seasonings, soups/soup preparations). Consider **Class 32** as well (non-alcoholic beverages, preparations for making beverages) — if you position as a *drink*, Class 32 is where a competitor could attack you.
- Fee: **₹4,500 per mark per class** for an individual/startup/MSME e-filing (this is why you do Udyam first); ₹9,000 otherwise.
- **Do the search before you commit to a name.** Use the IP India public search for wordmark and phonetic variants in Classes 30 and 32. "Rasa" and heritage-word names will likely be crowded. Budget ₹15,000–25,000 for an attorney-run clearance search across your shortlist — cheap insurance against rebranding after you've printed 50,000 envelopes.
- File as soon as you shortlist, on an intent-to-use basis. Registration takes 12–24 months but you can use ™ from filing.

| **Professional Tax** | If employees | State-level |

| **IEC (DGFT)** | Phase 3, export | ~₹500, online |
| **APEDA registration** | Phase 3, processed-food export | Required for export incentives |
| **Spices Board CRES** | Phase 3, if classified as spice export | Check applicability to your HSN |
| **FSSAI Central Licence** | Phase 3 | Mandatory for exporters |

## 8. Testing & documentation to budget for

You cannot print a label without these, and hotel/quick-commerce onboarding will ask for them:

- **Nutritional analysis panel** per SKU (NABL-accredited lab): ~₹8,000–15,000 per SKU
- **Microbiological + heavy metals + pesticide residue** panel per SKU: ~₹10,000–20,000 per SKU
- **Accelerated shelf-life study** to substantiate your 12-month claim: ~₹15,000–30,000 per SKU
- **Batch-wise COA** from your co-packer/unit, retained for traceability
- **Moisture content** verification per batch (in-house, cheap)

Three SKUs → budget **₹90,000–1,50,000** for the full first-round testing package. This is unavoidable and founders consistently forget it. It is in the capex line in `03-financials.md`.
