## 1. Three corrections you need before you spend anything

### (a) Hotels cannot be your first channel from a home kitchen

This is the one real conflict with your original plan, and it's better to hit it now than at month 4.

Hotel purchase departments — including regional chains like Minerva and Avira — will not onboard a **home-premises food business** as a vendor. Their vendor registration asks for a licensed commercial premises, usually a State Licence, often ISO 22000/HACCP, and frequently a physical audit. A home kitchen with a Basic Registration fails that gate before anyone tastes the product. It is not a taste problem or a selling problem; it is a document problem, and no amount of warm introduction fixes it.

**What actually works, in order:**

| Order | Channel | Will they buy from a licensed home producer? |
|---|---|---|
| **1st** | **Specialty / plant-based cafés** | **Yes.** They need your FSSAI number on the invoice. That's it. Fast payment, high margin, and they put you on a menu. |
| **2nd** | **Gyms, yoga & pilates studios** | **Yes.** Owner-operated, one decision maker, no procurement. |
| **3rd** | **Corporate pantries, co-working spaces** | **Mostly.** Smaller firms yes; large ones will ask for more. |
| **4th** | **Hotels — events & banquets** | **Sometimes**, via the Exec Chef as a one-off event supply rather than a standing vendor. This is your foot in the door. |
| **5th** | **Hotels — standing amenity/banquet contracts** | **Only via a co-packer's licensed premises.** Month 4+. |
| **6th** | Quick commerce (Blinkit/Zepto) | Needs GST + full labelling + lab panels. Month 7+. |

**This does not kill your hotel thesis — it delays it by one quarter and makes it cheaper.** When you get there, you don't buy a unit; you rent capacity from an FSSAI-licensed co-packer and invoice under their premises licence. That converts a ₹20L+ problem into a per-kg job-work charge.

Meanwhile, cafés do the visibility job you wanted hotels for — arguably better. A "Savoury Shot — ₹90" on a Jubilee Hills café menu is seen by exactly the Gen-Z, non-coffee, plant-based crowd you're targeting. A hotel amenity tray is seen by business travellers in their 40s.

### (b) Your capacity number assumes more hands than you have

Hand-packing one sachet means: weigh 3 g → fill the bag → heat-seal the bag → insert into the envelope → seal the envelope → apply the label. Working in batches (weigh 100, fill 100, seal 100 — never one at a time), one person sustains roughly **600–1,000 sachets/day**.

So your 6 kg/day (2,000 sachets) needs **2–3 people**, not one. Working alone or with one helper, plan for **10,000–15,000 sachets/month**, not 50,000. That is fine — it's enough for 15–20 café and gym accounts — but price and promise against the real number. Over-committing to a buyer you can't supply is the fastest way to lose an account permanently.

### (c) Your existing FSSAI registration may have quietly expired

FSSAI moved to **perpetual validity** on 1 April 2026 — but only for licences and registrations **applied for on or after that date**. Anything issued before then still runs on the old 1–5 year periodic validity. If your home registration predates April 2026, **it has a expiry date and may already be lapsed.** Separately, the **annual fee** and the **annual Food Safety Compliance Report** are now mandatory, and missing either **auto-suspends** the registration.

**Do this in week 1, it's free:** go to the **FoSCoS portal** → **FBO Search**, enter your business name, state and district. You'll get your licence number, premises address, validity status (Active/Inactive) and — importantly — **the product categories it covers.**

Check three things, not one:
1. **Is it Active?** If lapsed, re-apply rather than renew (a fresh application dated after 1 April 2026 gets perpetual validity).
2. **Does the premises address match where you'll actually produce?** Selling from an address that isn't on the licence is a live compliance exposure.
3. **Does the product category cover a manufactured seasoning/condiment blend?** A registration taken out as a retailer, reseller or for a different food category **does not cover manufacturing this product.** If it doesn't, file a modification. This is the single most likely thing to be wrong, and it's cheap to fix now and expensive to be caught on later.

## 2. GST is mandatory for you — and here's the specific reason

You are based in Andhra Pradesh and selling into Hyderabad (Telangana). That is **inter-state supply of goods**, which requires GST registration **regardless of turnover** under Section 24 of the CGST Act. The ₹40 lakh threshold does not apply to you.

Do it in week 1. It also lets you claim input tax credit on packaging and ingredients, which at your margins is real money, and no café or corporate will raise a PO to an unregistered vendor.

Confirm the **HSN 2103** classification (mixed condiments/seasonings, now **5% GST**) in writing with your CA before you print any MRP — see `02-compliance-india.md` §5.

## 5. Week-by-week

### Weeks 1–2 · Fix the paperwork, prove the format
1. **FoSCoS → FBO Search.** Recover the licence number. Check Active status, premises address, and product category (§1c). File a modification if the category doesn't cover manufactured seasonings.
2. **Apply for GST** (mandatory — inter-state, §2). **Apply for Udyam** the same day; it's free and it halves your trademark fee.
3. **Brief your CA:** confirm HSN 2103 / 5% in writing.
4. **Formulation:** 3 flavours × 3 iterations. Source **rice-flour-compounded hing**, not wheat-compounded — see the gluten flag in `01-product-packaging.md` §5. Source soluble tamarind extract powder.
5. **Free trademark search** on IP India public search, Classes 30 + 32, across a 4-name shortlist (`00-strategy.md` §6).

### Weeks 3–4 · The bake-off
6. **Blind test:** flat filter bag vs pyramid mesh vs stick pack vs home-made rasam, 10 tasters, scored on flavour intensity / body / aroma / "would you pay ₹90 for this in a café".
7. Lock the two formats you're shipping (you've chosen both — the test decides *which bag* and whether the stick pack needs a different grind).
8. Time yourself hand-packing 100 sachets in batches. Get your real per-day rate (§1b). Every promise you make to a buyer depends on this number.
9. **→ GATE 1**

### Weeks 5–6 · Get sellable
10. Brand + label artwork. Front of pack carries the stat block: **`0 SUGAR · 0 CAFFEINE · 0 DAIRY · ~6 KCAL`**. No health claims (`02-compliance-india.md` §2).
11. Print digital labels. Include 200 with a **mocked Minerva/Avira-style custom label** — not to sell yet, but to show cafés and gyms you're a real operation, and to have ready when a hotel chef asks "could this carry our logo?"
12. Pack 3,000 sachets. Build 40 sample kits.
13. **Build the target list:** 25 Hyderabad specialty/plant-based cafés (Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills, Gachibowli, Madhapur), 10 Vijayawada/Vizag cafés, 15 gyms and yoga/pilates studios, 5 co-working spaces.

### Weeks 7–8 · Sell
14. **30 in-person tastings.** Never pitch before they taste. Kettle, clear glass, three flavours, let them dip the bag themselves (`04-gtm-execution.md` §1).
15. The café ask is a **menu listing at ₹90**, not a cupboard order. Make the trade price conditional on the listing.
16. Gyms: lead with **beetroot and lemon** as savoury electrolytes.
17. **Ask Deepthi and Saketh for one named introduction each** — but to a **café owner or an Exec Chef for a one-off event**, not a hotel purchase desk. You can't service a purchase desk yet, and burning a warm intro on a meeting you'll fail the paperwork for is worse than not asking.
18. **→ GATE 2**

### Weeks 9–13 · Prove it repeats, and set up Phase 2
19. Chase **repeat orders**. Three repeats matter more than ten first-time orders — repeats are the only evidence the occasion is real.
20. Send the hero SKU for lab panels.
21. File the Class 30 trademark.
22. Launch Instagram: 30 posts across the three pillars in `04-gtm-execution.md` §5. Zero paid spend this quarter — organic and café-sourced content only.
23. **Shortlist 3 FSSAI-licensed co-packers** in Hyderabad/Vijayawada with teabag or sachet capability. Get job-work rates per kg. **This is your unlock for hotels** — don't wait until you need it.
24. **Land one hotel event supply** through a chef relationship — a single conference or a wedding tea break, invoiced as a one-off. That gives you the reference and the photos that make the month-4 chain conversation possible.

### Day-90 success criteria
- Format validated in blind test against home-made rasam
- **10 paying accounts**, of which **≥3 have reordered**
- **≥5 café menus** carrying the product
- Blended realisation **≥ ₹8.50/sachet** — you did not buy your way in on price (`03-financials.md` §3)
- Month-3 revenue **≥ ₹70,000**
- FSSAI Active, correct premises, correct category; GST live; Udyam done; trademark filed
- Three co-packer quotes in hand

## 6. Regional specifics — Andhra + Hyderabad

**Cafés (your volume and visibility engine).** Hyderabad's specialty-coffee and plant-based scene is concentrated in Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills, Madhapur and Gachibowli. Target independents, not chains — an owner can add a menu item in one conversation. Vijayawada and Visakhapatnam have smaller but genuinely growing café scenes with far less competition for menu space and far more willingness to try something new.

**Hotels (month 4+).** Your network maps unusually well here: Minerva Grand operates across multiple cities in this exact geography — Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, Tirupati among them (verify the current property list). That's the ideal structure: **land one property, prove consumption, then go to the chain's F&B head for all of them.** One chain conversation can be worth 4–6 properties. Keep this warm through weeks 9–13 via chef relationships and event supply, and open it properly once a co-packer gives you a licensed premises to invoice from.

**Labs.** Hyderabad has NABL-accredited food testing capacity — Vimta Labs is the best-known local option. Get three quotes; prices for the same panel vary by 40%+, and on a ₹22,000 line that's real money.

**Logistics.** Andhra → Telangana is inter-state: you need GST (§2) and e-way bills above the consignment threshold. Keep your first accounts geographically tight — one city at a time — so a delivery isn't a day trip.
