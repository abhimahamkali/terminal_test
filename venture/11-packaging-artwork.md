## 1. The correction: you cannot print "0 g sugar"

Writing the real nutrition panel exposed a flaw in the hook from `07-naming-and-hook.md`.

Under the **FSS (Advertising and Claims) Regulations 2018**, a *"sugar free"* / *"zero sugar"* claim
requires **no more than 0.5 g of sugars per 100 g**. Tamarind carries natural sugars — the blend lands
around **4.2 g per 100 g**, failing the threshold roughly eightfold.

There is a legitimate route. As **prepared**, one 3 g bag in 150 ml gives about **0.06 g sugars per
100 ml** — comfortably inside the limit. But a claim pegged to the prepared drink must be **declared
on that basis and backed by lab data**, which you won't have until the panels return in week 9.

| | |
|---|---|
| ~~`0 g SUGAR · 0 mg CAFFEINE · 0 DAIRY · ~6 KCAL`~~ | **Withdrawn** |
| `NO ADDED SUGAR · CAFFEINE FREE · NO DAIRY` | **Use this** |

"No added sugar" is unconditionally true, permitted, and costs you nothing — it's the exact phrase
the zero-sugar category was built on. Once the lab confirms prepared figures, add *"less than 0.1 g
sugar per cup"*, which reads as measured rather than claimed and is more persuasive than a round zero.

**Also corrected:** `~6 KCAL` is the as-prepared figure; as sold a 3 g bag is **8.6 kcal**. Write
**"under 10 kcal a cup"** until lab-confirmed.

## 2. Three more claim traps found while drafting

| Issue | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Don't print "vegan"** | The **FSS (Vegan Foods) Regulations 2022** gate the word behind approval and the official vegan logo. Say **"no dairy"** or **"plant-based"** until certified. |
| **"Low calorie" fails** | The threshold applies per 100 g **as sold**. At 285 kcal/100 g the powder fails, even though a cup is 6 kcal. |
| **Sodium is your exposed number** | **294 mg per bag** ≈ 15% of the 2,000 mg daily reference. Fine for one cup; awkward if content encourages three a day. It is the one figure a wellness buyer will hunt for. **Decide now: cut salt in the blend, or never build a "drink it all day" message.** |
| **Legal Metrology exemption ≠ FSSAI exemption** | The institutional pack escapes MRP and retail declarations. It still needs the full FSSAI set: name, ingredients, nutrition, allergens, licence number, manufacturer, batch, best-before. |

## 7. Compliance element key

| # | Element | Required by | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FSSAI logo + 14-digit licence number | Labelling & Display Regs 2020 | You don't have the number yet |
| 2 | Green vegetarian mark | Packaging & Labelling — veg symbol | Fixed geometry; don't let a designer restyle it |
| 3 | Name of food + category | Labelling & Display 2020 | The *legal* name, small type. "Savoury Brew" is the brand descriptor, not this |
| 4 | Brand name | — | Provisional. No print cylinder before the trademark screen clears |
| 5 | Claims / stat block | Advertising & Claims 2018 | See §1 and §2 |
| 6 | Ingredients, descending by weight | Labelling & Display 2020 | Compound ingredients need their own breakdown; declare the hing carrier |
| 7 | Allergen declaration | Labelling & Display 2020 | Rice-flour hing keeps you gluten-free; wheat-compounded would force a gluten declaration |
| 8 | Nutritional information | Labelling & Display 2020 | Per 100 g + per serving mandatory; as-prepared optional but it enables per-cup claims |
| 9 | Batch/lot, MFD, best before | Labelling & Display 2020 + Legal Metrology | Leave a clear overprint window; hand-stamping won't survive volume |
| 10 | Name + full address of FBO | Labelling & Display 2020 | **Must match the licence premises** |
| 11 | Net quantity | Legal Metrology 2011 | Count and weight; fill tolerance 3 g ± 0.15 g |
| 12 | Country of origin | Labelling & Display 2020 | Also needed for any export pack |
| 13 | Consumer care details | Legal Metrology 2011 | Working email and phone, not a social handle |
| 14 | MRP incl. all taxes + unit sale price | Legal Metrology 2011 | Retail pack only |
| 15 | "For institutional use only — not for retail sale" | Legal Metrology 2011 — institutional exemption | This marking *is* the exemption |

**Open item:** the reduced declaration set on the 3 g sachet relies on the **small-package
provision**. The exact size threshold and which declarations may be dropped are specific — confirm
once with a labelling consultant, then reuse forever.

## 8. Claims audit

| Claim | Status | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `NO ADDED SUGAR` | ✅ Use | Unconditionally true; permitted nutrient content claim |
| `CAFFEINE FREE` | ✅ Use | Naturally caffeine free |
| `NO DAIRY` | ✅ Use | Factual absence claim; your wedge into plant-based cafés |
| `NO ADDED MSG` | ✅ Use | Neutralises the main objection to Indian instant savoury products |
| `0 g SUGAR` / `SUGAR FREE` | ❌ Not on the powder | Needs ≤0.5 g/100 g; blend is ~4.2 g. Prepared basis only, with lab data |
| `~6 KCAL` | ⚠️ Qualify | As-prepared figure. As sold, 8.6 kcal. Use "under 10 kcal a cup" |
| `VEGAN` | ❌ Needs approval | Vegan Foods Regs 2022 — approval + official logo |
| `LOW CALORIE` | ❌ Fails | Threshold is per 100 g as sold; 285 kcal fails |
| `AIDS DIGESTION` `DEBLOATS` `GUT HEALTH` `APPETISING` | ❌ Hard stop | Health claims; risks the nutraceutical regime |
| `NATURAL` `PURE` `FRESH` | ❌ Restricted | Each separately restricted |
| Naming tamarind, pepper, cumin, hing in the ingredient story | ✅ Unlimited | Naming ingredients is not a claim. The deck implies; you never state |

## 9. Print specification

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Sachet envelope | 58 × 78 mm, 3-side seal, **PET / MET-PET / PE metallised laminate** — moisture barrier non-negotiable |
| Designs | **One** generic cylinder set for all three flavours. Rotogravure is ₹25–40k per cylinder with ~50,000-envelope MOQ *per design* |
| Flavour variation | Spot-colour band + digitally printed label |
| Hotel co-branding | Digital adhesive label at packing, MOQ ~1,000, ₹0.40–0.90 each |
| Overprint window | Clear unprinted panel, same position on all three formats; thermal transfer coder |
| Colour | Beet is the content hero — protect its saturation in print |
| Fill tolerance | 3 g ± 0.15 g |
| Shelf life | 12 months, to be validated by accelerated study |

### Five gates before artwork release
1. FSSAI number recovered **and** premises address verified
2. Product category on the licence confirmed to cover a manufactured seasoning
3. HSN opinion in writing (2103 @ 5%)
4. Trademark screen cleared on the chosen name
5. NABL lab panels received, so the nutrition table is real

**All five. Any one missing means a reprint.**
